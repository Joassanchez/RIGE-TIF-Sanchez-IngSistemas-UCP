Managed settings override everything.</p>\n<div class=\"aside\">\n<div class=\"aside-t\">Note</div>\n<p>The <code>.opencode</code> and <code>~/.config/opencode</code> directories use <strong>plural names</strong> for subdirectories: <code>agents/</code>, <code>commands/</code>, <code>modes/</code>, <code>plugins/</code>, <code>skills/</code>, <code>tools/</code>, and <code>themes/</code>.
